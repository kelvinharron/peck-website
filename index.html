FAQ

Peck speaks English, Español, Català, Français and Português — inside the app too.

Do I need to weigh everything?

No. Describe it roughly (“chicken salad, olive oil dressing”) or snap a photo and Peck estimates. Weigh when you want precision; otherwise log roughly and stay consistent.

What if the AI estimate is wrong?

Edit it in a tap — calories and each macro are adjustable. Peck saves your version.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. There is no account, so there is no cloud profile to breach or sell.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Export your history whenever you like, straight from the app.

Is Peck free?

Peck is free to download and track with. Peck Plus unlocks ad-free estimation and exclusive widgets.

Is Android supported?

Not yet — iOS first, Google Play is coming soon.
